Real Driving Feel

Weight transfer, grip loss under handbrake, centrifugal push through corners, off-road drag. The car has to be driven, not just steered.

Nitro & Drift Scoring

Hold Shift to boost. Tap Space to kick the rear out. Drifts and near-misses build a combo multiplier that multiplies every point.

Progression

How you climb

Points and XP are both earned every run, but they do different jobs — one spends, one levels.

Scoring in a run

Distance

Points for every metre covered on the road. Off-road metres pay far less.

Drift

Hold a slide to bank points per second. The longer and faster the drift, the more it pays.

Near miss

Squeeze past traffic and barriers for a bonus plus a combo bump.

Checkpoints & clean finish

Each checkpoint pays out, and finishing with health left adds a large bonus.

Spending & levelling

Points → Garage

Points are the currency. Buy a car once and it stays in your garage forever.

XP → Driver Rank

XP raises your rank: Rookie all the way to VSCARZ Champion. Ranks are permanent.

Rank → Tracks

Each track needs a minimum rank. Rank 12 opens the Grand Prix finale.

Records

Your best score per track and per car is stored, so you always know the number to beat.
